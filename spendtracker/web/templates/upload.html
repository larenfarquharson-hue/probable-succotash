The layout is detected automatically: preamble rows, header wording, one signed amount column or separate debit/credit columns, day-first or month-first dates. Where the file has a running balance, the amount signs are checked against it and corrected if the convention is inverted.

Re-importing an overlapping date range is safe. Identical rows are recognised and skipped; anything the data cannot prove either way goes to Review and is held out of your totals until you decide.
